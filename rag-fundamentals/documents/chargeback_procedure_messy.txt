CHARGEBACK   HANDLING   PROCEDURE
Acme Payments  ·  Risk Operations          Page 1 of 2


A chargeback (also called a dispute) happens when a customer
asks their bank to reverse a card payment.   The bank removes
the money from the merchant's balance immediately and opens an
investi-
gation.


Merchants have	10 days	to respond to a chargeback
with evidence,   such as:
	-  proof of delivery
	-  the customer's signed order or
	   accepted terms
	-  records of communication with
	   the customer


If the merchant does not respond within 10 days, the bank
decides in the customer's favour automatically.

Each chargeback carries a non-refundable fee,   even if the
merchant wins the dispute.   Merchants whose chargeback rate
goes above 1% of transactions in a month are placed on a
monitoring programme and may have their payouts delayed.
